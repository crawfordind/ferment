Open Q · header hierarchy — pick one
A (photo-dominant): the jar is the hero, banner is a one-liner. Best for "match the container / read drift at a glance."  B (banner-dominant): the day's expectation leads. Best for "what do I do today." My lean: A for daily scanning, since the photo also carries identity — but B is stronger early in a batch when guidance matters most. Could even switch automatically by status.
Finishing a batch → next steps
"Move container" keeps the same record (re-jar / decant). "Start secondary" opens New Batch pre-linked to this one as its parent — that's batch lineage, pulled forward from the PRD's future list. Worth confirming you want lineage in v1.
