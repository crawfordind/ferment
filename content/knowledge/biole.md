## What it is

A Bio-ferment (Biole) is a fermented liquid organic fertilizer: liquid organic materials worked
on by Mountain Microorganisms — yeast, fungi and bacteria — which transform them into plant
elements, vitamins, organic acids and other growth substances. Sprayed on the crop, the
nutrients are absorbed through the leaves right away. It is a microbial soup that re-establishes
the ecosystem's microbial balance and makes plants more resistant to pests and disease.

> **On the Nutritive Cycle:** Biole is an *all-stages* input — a balanced backbone tonic that
> nourishes crops and steadies soil microbial life throughout the season.

## When to use it

- **Any growth stage** — a foliar tonic to nourish crops and boost resistance.
- **Dilution** — **1 L bio-ferment : 1 L water** (or 10 L : 10 L) in a 20 L spray pump.
- **Before use** — shake the container well for a uniform mix; open, measure what you need, and re-seal immediately.

## Materials

- Fresh milk — **2 L** (or **4 L** whey)
- Fresh cow dung — **10 kg** (un-vaccinated cow, kept out of sun)
- Molasses — **2 L**; wood ash — **1 kg**
- Activated MM solution — **2 L**; clean water — **20 L**
- 30 L drum, metal ring fastener, hose and water bottle for the airlock

## How to make it

1. **Load the drum.** Put in **2 L milk**, then **10 kg fresh cow dung**.
2. **Sweeten and add ash.** Dilute **2 L molasses in 20 L water**, add it plus **1 kg wood ash**.
3. **Add the microbes.** Stir in **2 L activated MM** until uniform; pick out wood chips. No acidic juices.
4. **Seal.** Fasten the lid airtight with the metal ring.
5. **Airlock.** Run a hose from the lid into a bottle of water so gases bubble out and no oxygen returns.
6. **Wait 25 days.** Keep under shelter, away from sun and rain. Do not open before **day 25**.

## Signs it worked / troubleshooting

- ✅ **Good:** steady gas bubbles rising through the water-bottle airlock as it ferments.
- ⚠️ **No bubbles** = poor seal or weak MM → check the lid and airlock are airtight.
- 🚫 **Opened early or contaminated with acidic juice** = spoiled batch → keep it sealed the full 25 days and avoid citrus.

## How to store

Keep the sealed drum under shelter, protected from sun and rain. Ready at **25 days**; open only when needed and re-close immediately. Apply diluted **1:1** with water.
