## What it is

Seawater carries the full spread of ocean **minerals** — regarded in Natural Farming as essential
alongside NPK. Left in a bowl for a day, it picks up **airborne microbes**; when land microbes meet
sea minerals and microbes the result is a fertile, brackish energy source that feeds crops and
livestock. Rice-washed water and FPJ deepen the effect.

> **On the Nutritive Cycle:** Seawater is both a *reproductive* input — it sweetens ripening fruit —
> and an *all-stages* mineral and microbial tonic for soil and stock.

## When to use it

- **Sweeten fruit** — add **WS-Ca** to diluted seawater, spray **twice in one month**, then again **20 days before harvest**.
- **General mineral feed** — dilute **1 L seawater : 30 L water** (sun-dried salt is instead 2 ml per 1000 ml).
- **Soil application** — apply sun-dried salt at **5 kg per 10 acres**.
- **Livestock** — to prevent summer feather loss in chickens, mix BRV (200) and WS-Ca (500) into diluted seawater and feed once every two days.

## Materials

- Surface seawater — **1 L** per **30 L** of dilution water
- A large bowl for aging
- Optional: rice-washed water and FPJ (**5 ml** each), WS-Ca, OHN

## How to make it

1. **Skim the surface.** The deeper the sea, the higher the salt — so collect surface seawater.
2. **Age one day.** Pour it into a large bowl and leave it a day to let airborne microbes go in.
3. **Enrich (optional).** Add rice-washed water and FPJ at **5 ml** each; rice-washed water acts like yeast, and OHN makes it stronger still.
4. **Dilute to use.** Mix **1 L** of seawater into **30 L** of water before applying.

## Signs it worked / troubleshooting

- ✅ **Good:** sweeter, more aromatic fruit after seawater-plus-WS-Ca sprays; healthy, well-feathered stock.
- ⚠️ **Leaf or salt burn** = too concentrated → hold the **1:30** dilution and don't over-apply salt.
- 🚫 **Foul, rotten smell in stored seawater** = spoiled → discard and collect fresh surface water.

## How to store

Keep the aged seawater cool and shaded. Use diluted batches promptly rather than storing them long.
